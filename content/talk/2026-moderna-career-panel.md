Panel discussion at the [SAPA-NE x Moderna 2026 Career Development Workshop Part 2](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/sapa-ne-x-moderna-2026-career-development-workshop-part-2-sapane-onv8e/) on owning your career narrative in a changing biotech industry.

Themes covered:

- **Strategic visibility and personal branding**: doing strong work quietly is no longer enough. Three content pillars for building a brand: share past achievements, discuss current work or learning, and comment thoughtfully on industry developments.
- **AI and the future of scientific work**: build AI fluency now, but scientists remain fully responsible for data accuracy, interpretation, and regulatory integrity. AI outputs must always be verified.
- **Leadership readiness**: the shift from thinking only about scientific questions to considering business implications, strategic trade-offs, and program-level impact.
- **Business acumen for scientists**: ask three recurring questions of any project: What is the technology and data really telling us? What is the unique value proposition? What is the best strategic path to achieve the goal?
