![RSG Pakistan bioinformatics career talk](/img/RSG-parkistan.jpeg)

Morning session with [RSG Pakistan](https://www.iscbsc.org/rsg/regional-student-groups), the official Pakistani chapter of the International Society for Computational Biology Student Council, sharing my bioinformatics journey and career stories.

More than 80 Pakistani students and professionals joined online. One of them is studying in Kunming, China, and he spoke to me in Chinese during the Q&A.

I was once a beginner. I still cannot quite believe how far the road has gone. If I could do it, they can too.
